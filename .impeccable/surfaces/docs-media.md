THESIS: Coach is the coach writing on your own training log. Every visual is a spiral log page: the user's entries in pencil, the coach's corrections in red felt-tip carrying the numbers. Refuses the dark-terminal-plus-tagline dev banner and the neon-athlete gym poster.
